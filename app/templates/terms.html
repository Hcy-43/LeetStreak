Terms of use

Last updated 3 September 2026.

LeetStreak shows your public LeetCode activity next to your friends', so a group can keep each other honest about practising daily. It is free, and it is run as a side project rather than a business.

Using it

  • Use your own LeetCode account. Each account can be claimed once.
  • Groups are private. Only share an invite link with people you want on your board.
  • Do not use this to harass anyone, and do not try to break into other people's accounts or groups.

What you can expect

It runs on free hosting and may be slow, briefly unavailable, or occasionally behind on data. It is provided as-is, with no warranty and no guarantee that it will keep running. Keep nothing here you would be upset to lose — the real record of your solutions lives on LeetCode.

Your account

You can leave a group or ask for your account to be deleted at any time. Accounts that abuse the service may be removed.

Not affiliated with LeetCode

LeetStreak is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to LeetCode. It only reads the public profile data LeetCode already shows to anyone.
