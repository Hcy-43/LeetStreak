Privacy

Last updated 3 September 2026.

LeetStreak is a small side project run by one person for a group of friends. It is not a company and it does not sell anything. This page describes exactly what it stores and who can see it.

What is stored about you

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and to send verification codes.
  • A hash of your password, if you set one. The password itself is never stored and cannot be recovered from the hash.
  • Your LeetCode username, and the daily counts of solved problems read from it.
  • Your display name and timezone.
  • If you sign in with Google: the account identifier Google gives us, plus the name and profile picture on that account.
  • Which groups you belong to.

Where the LeetCode data comes from

We read the public submission calendar from your LeetCode profile — the same green squares anyone can see by visiting it. We never ask for your LeetCode password and have no access to your account.

Who can see it

Your squares and streak are visible to the people in a group with you, and to nobody else. There is no public leaderboard, no directory, and no way to browse other people's boards. Someone who signs up but joins no group sees only their own.

Who else is involved

  • Render hosts the application.
  • Neon hosts the database.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with it.
  • Gmail delivers verification codes.
  • LeetCode, whose public API we read.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies — the only cookies are the one that keeps you signed in, a short-lived one during sign-up, and your light/dark preference.

Deleting your data

Email the address at the bottom of this page and your account and all of its activity will be deleted. Leaving a group removes your board from it immediately.

Honestly

This is a hobby project on free hosting. It is built carefully — passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions are signed, boards are private to their groups — but please do not treat it as critical infrastructure, and do not reuse a password here that protects anything important.
